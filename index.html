About

From Kildare and now based in Boston, Finbar Doyle is a singer and guitarist who plays pubs, festivals and charity nights on both sides of the Atlantic, from Lisdoonvarna to Providence.

“It doesn’t matter what they do, it matters what you do. Be kind, be you.”
